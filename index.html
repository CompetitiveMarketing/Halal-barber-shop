Before you drop in

A few
good answers.

Keep it simple. Here’s what to know before your next visit.

Do I need to book an appointment?

No appointment is needed. Halal Barbers welcomes walk-ins. For a specific enquiry before coming in, call 0474 107 251.

Are the prices the same at every shop?

Each shop has its own menu. Select Butler, Joondalup or Bassendean in the services section to see that location’s prices, including the complete menu.

Do you do beard trims and haircut combinations?

Yes. Beard services are available across the shops. Joondalup and Bassendean also list haircut and beard combinations. Check your selected shop’s menu for options.

Can I bring a photo of the cut I want?

Of course. A reference photo helps you explain the length, shape and finish you have in mind. Talk it through with your barber before the cut.

Are you open on Sundays?

All three locations list Sunday trading hours. Hours differ between shops, and Sunday or public-holiday surcharges apply. Check your chosen location and call ahead around public holidays.
